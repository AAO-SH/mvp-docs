# Dispute bonds and governance waivers

AAO requires a refundable Dispute Bond or pre-reserved dispute-cost Budget
Allocation before a Dispute Case can block settlement, payment, reputation,
budget release, assignment, or review. Only DAO-governed authority can waive
that bond requirement by default; private-company owners do not receive a free
blocking-dispute path because it would let payers delay executor settlement at
no cost. Frivolous, abusive, unsupported, spammy, or bad-faith disputes may
forfeit the bond through Settlement Lines for resolver costs, affected-party
costs, or treasury recovery, and that forfeiture is not agent Slashing.
